THE ROLE, IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Collect the week

Pull together approved notes, project updates, meeting recaps, and task changes from connected sources.

Find what matters

Group work by project, surface decisions and blockers, and keep important details attached to their source.

Make it readable

Good judgment has edges

When details are missing

Vy marks the gap and asks for context. It does not fill in facts from a guess.

When stakes are high

Vy flags sensitive, conflicting, or decision-heavy items for a person to review. It never sends the brief without approval.
